# 📊 Clicker Check

What year did Codd publish *A Relational Model of Data for Large Shared Data Banks*?

A. 1965
B. 1970
C. 1976
D. 1981

<!--
Answer: B. 1970, in Communications of the ACM. Codd won the Turing Award for this work in 1981 (option D is the Turing Award year). 1976 (option C) is Chen's ER paper.
-->

---

# Clicker Check — Answer

**B. 1970.**

Codd published in *Communications of the ACM* in June 1970. He won the **Turing Award for this work in 1981**.

The Chen ER paper (option C, 1976) and System R (1974-79) followed. The whole relational era cascaded from the 1970 paper.

# 📊 Clicker Check

<!-- _backgroundColor: #fff8e1 -->

What language was UNIX (Bell Labs, 1969) **initially** implemented in?

A. C
B. Assembly
C. Fortran
D. Pascal

<!--
Answer: B. UNIX was originally written in assembly. C was created in 1972 by Dennis Ritchie *to rewrite UNIX in a portable way*. The portability of UNIX once it was rewritten in C is one of the reasons it became universal.
-->

---

# Clicker Check — Answer

**B. Assembly.**

UNIX was originally written in PDP-7 assembly by Thompson and Ritchie. The need to port it to other hardware led Ritchie to design **C** in 1972 — specifically to make a portable systems language.

Once UNIX was rewritten in C (1973), it could be ported to any machine with a C compiler. That portability is part of why UNIX became the universal substrate every modern database runs on.
